# SPE Technical Evaluation Report: Day-by-Day Autonomous Design Configuration D3_S0_A5

## 1. Executive Summary & Winning System Architecture

The D3_S0_A5 configuration represents a cutting-edge geothermal energy system designed to maximize efficiency, reliability, and sustainability. This configuration employs three doublets, which are strategically positioned to optimize geothermal resource extraction while ensuring operational flexibility. With a total storage capacity of 25 MWh and a high coverage percentage of 99.99%, the system is engineered to meet peak demand with minimal reliance on auxiliary gas, recorded at only 0.014 GWh. 

The well production rates are robust, with two active wells yielding 84.4 GWh and 43.33 GWh, respectively, while a third well serves as a backup, enhancing operational resilience. The mean flow rates of the wells, ranging from 211.2 to 233.9 m³/h, are sufficient to sustain production during peak demand periods. The architecture is designed to accommodate seasonal variations in thermal loads, with a high-temperature heat pump (HTHP) rated at 6.0 MW, ensuring efficient operation throughout the year.

## 2. Subsurface Production Dynamics

The subsurface dynamics of the D3_S0_A5 configuration are characterized by the performance of its three doublets. The production profiles are as follows:

- **Well 1**: Active for 330 days, producing 84.4 GWh with a mean flow rate of 233.9 m³/h.
- **Well 2**: Active for 178 days, producing 43.33 GWh with a mean flow rate of 222.7 m³/h.
- **Well 3**: Primarily a backup, active for only 5 days, producing 1.15 GWh with a mean flow rate of 211.2 m³/h.

The operational strategy allows for flow modulation between 50 to 300 m³/h, enabling the system to adapt to varying demand scenarios. The active/inactive days of each well are optimized to ensure continuous energy production while allowing for maintenance and recovery periods, thus enhancing the overall sustainability of the geothermal resource.

## 3. Temperature Control & Cascada Strategy

The temperature control strategy is pivotal in maximizing the efficiency of the geothermal system. The configuration utilizes an 87 °C brine preheat, which is then boosted by a high-temperature heat pump (HTHP) to reach 100 °C, ensuring optimal thermal output for energy generation. 

The reinjection strategy is designed to maintain reservoir sustainability, with reinjection temperatures exceeding 47 °C. This approach not only preserves the geothermal resource but also enhances the thermal efficiency of the system, allowing for a closed-loop operation that minimizes thermal losses and maximizes energy recovery.

## 4. Surface Thermal Machinery Integration

The surface thermal machinery integration is a critical component of the D3_S0_A5 configuration. The system incorporates a high-temperature heat pump (HTHP) rated at 6.0 MW, which is essential for elevating the temperature of the geothermal fluid to meet energy demands. 

Additionally, the configuration employs a thermal energy storage (TES) system with a capacity of 25 MWh, allowing for the storage of excess thermal energy during low-demand periods. The integration of 100% absorption chilling technology ensures that the system can provide cooling solutions efficiently, further enhancing the versatility of the geothermal energy system.

## 5. Techno-Economic & Lifecycle OPEX Analysis

The techno-economic analysis of the D3_S0_A5 configuration reveals a total capital expenditure (CAPEX) of €46.77 million. This investment is justified by the system's operational efficiency and low levelized cost of energy (LCOE), calculated at €54.19/MWh. 

The breakdown of CAPEX includes:

- Drilling and completion of wells
- Installation of surface equipment (HTHP, TES, chillers)
- Infrastructure development (piping, electrical systems)
- Environmental and regulatory compliance costs

The lifecycle operational expenditure (OPEX) is minimized through efficient resource management and the strategic use of backup wells, ensuring that operational costs remain low while maximizing energy output.

## 6. Conclusion & Digital Twin Verification

In conclusion, the D3_S0_A5 configuration stands out as an optimal geothermal energy solution, balancing cost, coverage, and operational efficiency while minimizing auxiliary gas usage. The robust production capabilities, combined with a strategic approach to temperature control and surface machinery integration, position this configuration as a leader in sustainable geothermal energy production.

To further validate the design and operational parameters, a digital twin of the system will be developed. This digital representation will allow for real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and optimization of the geothermal system, ensuring that it continues to meet performance expectations throughout its lifecycle.

The D3_S0_A5 configuration is not only a testament to innovative geothermal engineering but also a model for future developments in the field, paving the way for a sustainable energy future.
